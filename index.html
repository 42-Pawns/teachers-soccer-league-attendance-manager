Teachers' Soccer Team RSVP Notification Manager

This utility is an independent hobbyist project developed by Leon Perelman to run the attendance notifications for TPP Soccer League. It helps coordinate player attendance, schedules, and other updates for recreational soccer matches in the teachers' community.

How It Works

Active players explicitly opt-in by providing their mobile numbers to the coordinator or via team sign-up lists to receive automated availability checks prior to scheduled games.

SMS Privacy Policy & Disclosure

Opt-In and Pricing: By providing your phone number and requesting game notifications, you consent to receive automated transactional text messages (such as game availability requests and schedule updates). Message and data rates may apply. Frequency varies based on the team's match schedule.

Opt-Out: You can cancel the SMS service at any time. Just text "STOP" to our toll-free number. After you send the SMS message "STOP" to us, we will send you an SMS message to confirm that you have been unsubscribed. After this, you will no longer receive SMS messages from us.

Data Security: Your privacy is highly important to us. Mobile numbers collected for team communications and opt-in data will strictly not be shared, rented, or sold to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes under any circumstances.
